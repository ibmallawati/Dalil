. Part time students/ students on academic probation are to be counted on the basis of their
taking hours in relation to FTE students (maybe 12 or more).

. Full-time equivalent (FTE) instructor is considered for this indicator.

. FTE represents both full-time instructors and part-time instructors.

. Each full-time instructor is counted as one FTE and part-time instructors are to be computed on

the basis of their teaching hours (by credit) in relation to FTE teaching capacity (maybe 12 or
15 credits hours in several institutions).

. Contact hours have to converted to credits hours.

. Using different denominators in computing FTE of part time students and FTE of part time
instructors, i.e. different standards of institutions (minimum teaching requirement of students

and FTE workload standard of instructors) will give different ratios.
